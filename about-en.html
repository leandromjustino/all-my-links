About me

Who I am

I am a student dedicated to the original languages of the Bible and to Digital Humanities applied to theology. In my free time, I deepen my study of Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, and Latin, seeking to build the skills required for advanced research in biblical studies.

I study English to access specialized literature and broaden my academic reach. My main area of interest is New Testament Greek. I share my academic progress on social media and serve my local community through biblical teaching and communication.
